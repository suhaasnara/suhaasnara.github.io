About

I graduated summa cum laude from the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania with a B.S. in Economics, concentrating in Finance and in Statistics & Data Science. Most recently, I was a strategy consulting intern at Advancy in London, where I worked on market analysis and commercial due diligence projects for PE clients.

During college, I took two years away from Penn to work in venture capital at Innosphere Ventures in Fort Collins and coach chess. I've also interned with the City of Fort Collins, Ryder System and the Aravind Eye Care System in India.

Outside of work, I'm a USCF National Master. I've played chess since I was four, and I coach and direct tournaments in Fort Collins. You can read about my chess journey and coaching. I also enjoy meditation, climbing, and volleyball.
