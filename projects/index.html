Goals Scored in the Premier League

Is there a best of the best?

Read the full project →

The question

The top level in sports pits the best players against each other, and there's considerable money to scout and secure them. Are the best different from each other? These are common questions for any sports fan, but it is also a key business problem. Do the Lakers trade for Doncic and give up Anthony Davis in return? Is Haaland truly better than other scorers, or is he like the rest of the Premier League?

What I did

These are questions of heterogeneity, the differences between people. Goals are key count data to investigate this. Count data is frequently modeled using the Poisson distribution, but the Negative Binomial Distribution accounts for hyper dispersion of data, a key concern in many real-life data sets. I applied NBD models with varying time (minutes played) to the 2023-24 season, then looked at forwards and midfielders on their own. (Earlier datasets didn't make the cut. Giannis and Jokic score a lot of 2s.)

What I found

Across the full dataset, a low r indicates high heterogeneity, meaning the soccer players are different from one another. But the forwards and midfielders subgroup revealed a higher r (greater than 1), meaning they're similar to each other. Pretty good years for a striker may be a lucky spin of the Poisson wheel. Haaland may still be better than everyone else, but on the whole, the top forwards and midfielders may be more similar to each other than different.

Maze Therapeutics: Series E Investment

An investment proposal for Maze Therapeutics, a clinical-stage precision medicine startup.

Read the full project →

Why Maze

Maze's Compass™ platform integrates variant functionalization with big biobank data and AI, creating defensible intellectual property and accelerating pipeline development. Shionogi's $150M upfront and $600M milestones licensing deal validates Maze's asset quality, and our model projects risk-adjusted revenues reaching ~$3.7B by 2034.

The deal

Our valuation model triangulates value through a risk-adjusted DCF and comparables, and values Maze at $3.88 billion pre-money. We opened with a first offer term sheet at $13.60 per share for 4.0% ownership. Setting LP value equal to LP cost gives a breakeven of $10.00 per share, so a $160M investment secures at least 9% post-money ownership.

Predicting Real Estate Prices

A look into the Melbourne housing market, with about 6.2K sales after cleaning, more than 20 features, 287 suburbs, and prices from $131K to $9M.

The question

Which features drive house prices, and can we predict them accurately? Better predictions help sellers with pricing strategy and help investors make decisions about property value appreciation.

What we did

We started with multiple linear regression, which is simple and interpretable but suffers from heteroscedasticity. Lasso reduces variance but has a lower R² than the baseline. Then we tried black-box models (random forest, XGBoost, and neural networks) and used SHAP and PDP plots to see what drives them.

  • Linear regressionR² 0.74
  • LassoR² 0.71
  • Random forestR² 0.81
  • XGBoostR² 0.85

What we found

Machine learning models, specifically XGBoost, outperform traditional regression methods. Interpretability remains a challenge, but accuracy is prioritized. The most impactful predictors were council area, region, bathrooms, and rooms. Region and council area may capture location-based factors like infrastructure, desirability, zoning regulations, and economic activity.

Hurricane Katrina's Impact on Employment

What was the causal impact of Hurricane Katrina on county level economic outcomes in affected regions?

The natural experiment

Hurricane Katrina was one of the most severe natural disasters in U.S. history, causing extensive physical destruction, population displacement, and disruption to local labor markets across the Gulf Coast. The storm's path and intensity created sharp differences in exposure across counties that were largely unrelated to pre-existing economic conditions, generating plausibly exogenous variation in economic outcomes.

What we did

We compared affected and unaffected counties before and after the hurricane using difference-in-differences, with county-level quarterly employment data for Louisiana: a balanced sample of 64 counties over 20 quarters (1,280 observations). The model includes county and time fixed effects, with standard errors clustered at the county level. The key assumption is parallel trends: in the absence of Hurricane Katrina, employment trends would be similar across the affected and unaffected counties.

  • Affected counties, pre to post≈ -10,000 jobs
  • Unaffected counties, pre to post≈ +2,000 jobs
  • DiD estimate (county and time FE)≈ -12,800 jobs
  • p-value, clustered by county0.368

What we found

The regression estimated an average decrease of approximately 12,800 jobs in quarterly employment for counties affected by Hurricane Katrina. However, this point estimate isn't statistically significant, with the standard error higher than the estimated treatment effect. Visually, there is a noticeable decline, and interestingly, there seems to be a recovery in employment as time progresses.

The findings suggest there isn't a cleanly uniform impact on employment across affected counties. This may be due to heterogeneous effects, few clusters, and a limited post-period. Spillovers are a major limitation: unaffected counties may receive displaced workers or aid in recovery efforts.

LBO Proposal: DocuSign

A take-private LBO of DocuSign (NasdaqGS: DOCU) by Thoma Bravo.

Why DocuSign

We screened for companies above $1B in EV with LBO-favored characteristics: strong FCF (FCF margins above ~20%), decent EBITDA growth, and trading at significantly lower than all-time-high prices to find potentially mispriced assets. DocuSign is a market leader with a 50%+ share of the $10B e-signature market, robust and resilient cash flows, and substantial growth potential through its new Intelligent Agreement Management (IAM) platform. We chose Thoma Bravo for its focus on software, its experience with take-privates, and its track record of accelerating growth for enterprise SaaS businesses.

Value creation

Value creation will be driven by international expansion, IAM acceleration, cost optimization, and management synergies. By rationalizing the cost structure, accelerating IAM, and tailoring the go-to-market model for new global markets, EBITDA margins can be improved from the current 26-30% up to 45% in the long term.

Returns

The base case reaches a 25% IRR and a 5.9x MOIC, with exit options including a second IPO, a sale to a new financial sponsor, or a sale to a large strategic investor such as Microsoft. We built the LBO model, DCF, and deck.
