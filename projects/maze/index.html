Maze Therapeutics: Series E Investment

An investment proposal for Maze Therapeutics, a clinical-stage precision medicine startup: a valuation model, a pitch, two term sheets, and an internal investment memo.

Course
FNCE 2500, Venture Capital and the Finance of Innovation
Team
Team of five
When

Sources
PitchBook, Maze's S-1, company filings
The company

Precision medicine powered by genetics

Maze is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical startup, founded in 2018 by Dr. Jason Coloma in San Francisco. It makes small-molecule precision medicines for cardiovascular, renal, and metabolic indications. Maze turns naturally protective gene variants into small-molecule drugs that go beyond symptom relief to actually halt or reverse disease.

Variant discovery

Large-scale public (UK Biobank, etc.) and private genetic datasets identify genetic components linked to pathogenic and protective properties.

Variant to function

Functional genomics (CRISPR, cell models, etc.) specifies the impact of the variant and builds hypotheses on the mechanism of action.

Function to therapy

Small-molecule therapeutics that can be an oral therapy, validated in vivo and in vitro, with aggregated datasets used to select trial patients.

Why Maze

The investment thesis

Proprietary Compass™ platform

Maze's genetic discovery platform integrates variant functionalization with big biobank data and AI, creating defensible intellectual property and accelerating pipeline development.

De-risked pipeline and revenues

Shionogi's $150M upfront and $600M milestones licensing deal on MZE001 validates Maze's asset quality. Ongoing milestone payments and royalties provide future non-dilutive capital sources.

Large market opportunities

Maze's CKD therapies address a >$16B TAM by 2030, while precision treatment niches (APOL1, PKU) offer high pricing power and lower competitive density.

Seasoned management

The executive team brings senior experience from BioMarin, Roche, and Third Rock Ventures, providing operational expertise and M&A experience.

Revenue forecast

Risk-adjusted revenue, program by program

Each program is segmented by clinical development stage. Revenue from patient sales = TAM × market penetration × annual price × probability of success. Penetration assumptions are conservative (2-4%) for capture post-approval due to frictions in treatment adoption, and each revenue stream is risk-adjusted based on the likelihood of clinical success (22% for Phase 1, 30% for Phase 2, 70% for Phase 3).

Maze's forecasted revenue from 2028 to 2034 grows at a ~9.1% CAGR, led by the APOL1 kidney disease and CKD programs, with total risk-adjusted annual revenue projected to reach ~$3.7B by 2034.

Valuation

Four ways to value Maze

Our valuation model triangulates value through a combination of risk-adjusted DCF and comparables-based approaches. The biggest swing is the discount rate: a WACC of ~6% is based on comparables but is unlikely to capture startup risk, while a WACC of ~16% is based on the VC hurdle rate and the risk of startup ventures.

Scenario: One-Hit Wonder

Maze has success with one drug in its pipeline and doesn't continue to have successes, so there is no terminal value. This is common for smaller biotechs that are acquired for one key therapeutic, similar to precedent transactions.

DCF valuation: $976.2M

Scenario: Vertex Pharmaceuticals

Maze becomes a market player like Vertex with a successful pipeline, with a strong terminal value to reflect its growth potential and forecasted, probability-adjusted cash flows. Achieving a status like Vertex is a component of our thesis.

DCF valuation: $1,926.9M

The deal

From first offer to breakeven

We value Maze Therapeutics internally at $3.88 billion pre-money. To begin the negotiation, we drafted a first offer term sheet for a $160 million Series E: Series E Preferred Stock with a 1x non-participating liquidation preference and broad-based weighted average anti-dilution protection.

Where we'd give ground

Risks

What could go wrong

Clinical development

MZE829 and MZE782 are in Phase 2 and Phase 1. Setbacks in safety, efficacy, or trial enrollment could materially delay timelines.

Capital intensity

With a burn rate of ~$8.9M per month, Maze will likely need more financing before commercialization.

Competition

Vertex already has a late-stage APOL1 candidate. Maze's success depends on scientific differentiation and speed to market.

Platform scalability

Compass's credibility hinges on replicating variant functionalization beyond the initial kidney and metabolic targets.

Even under conservative success assumptions, Maze Therapeutics represents an attractive investment with both near-term and long-term value drivers.
